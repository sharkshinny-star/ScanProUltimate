In short
  • Your scans, photos, recognised text and documents stay on your phone. ScanPro has no account and no server of its own, and it does not show ads.
  • Google's ML Kit, which powers text recognition, barcode scanning, translation and the document-scanner fallback, sends Google small diagnostic reports. They never contain your images or text.
  • Translation downloads its language models over the internet. That is the only reason the app needs a network connection.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you share, export or email it yourself.

1. What ScanPro is

ScanPro is an offline-first document scanner for Android. It captures pages with the camera, tidies and crops them, recognises their text, builds PDFs and keeps a searchable library. Documents, page images, recognised text, notes, tags and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The core scanning workflow needs no account.

2. Camera, photos and files

The app asks for camera access when you scan. Camera frames are used on the device to find the page edges, judge blur and lighting, and take the picture. Photos and files are imported through Android's Photo Picker and file picker, so the app sees only what you choose. It does not ask for access to all your files. For PDFs you open from other apps, Android keeps a read permission for those files so that you can reopen them from Recent. You can remove them from that list at any time.

3. Text recognition and other features that run on your device

Text recognition, barcode and QR scanning, language detection, and the document-insight features (for example spotting a receipt total or a business card) run on your device. Recognised text is stored locally so you can search it, correct it, copy it and export it. Scanning a business card offers to open your Contacts app with the details filled in; ScanPro does not read your contacts. Scanning a Wi-Fi code can open Android's own "save this network" prompt; the password is never included when you share the code.

4. Google ML Kit and the diagnostic data it sends

ScanPro uses Google's ML Kit libraries for text recognition (Latin, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese and Korean), barcode scanning, language identification, translation and, on compatible phones, Google's document-scanner fallback. According to Google's ML Kit data disclosure, these libraries send Google the following over HTTPS, for diagnostics and usage analytics, and do not pass it to third parties:

WhatExamples
Device informationManufacturer, model, Android version and build
Application informationThe app's package name and version
A per-installation identifierUsed by Google to count installations, not to identify you
Performance and settingsHow long a recognition took, the image format and size it ran with, the library version
Events and errorsThat a model loaded, ran or failed, with an error code
Translation onlyThe languages you chose to translate from and to

This does not include your images, scans or recognised text, and the app has no setting that turns it off. It is declared in ScanPro's Google Play Data safety form as Diagnostics, Other app performance data, and Device or other IDs, collected for analytics. Google's own privacy policy applies to what Google receives.

5. Translation

If you use translation, ML Kit downloads the language model it needs. The text is then translated on your device. The download is the only network use in the app. The text you translate is not sent anywhere.

6. Where your documents are kept

Documents are stored in the app's private storage and indexed there for search. They stay available without an internet connection until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. The optional app lock uses your phone's biometrics or screen lock to control who can open the app. It is an access control, not a separate encryption of your library; the files rely on Android's own protection of app storage. ScanPro's data is excluded from Google's cloud backup, so it is never copied to a server that way. When you set up a new phone with Android's phone-to-phone transfer, your library and settings can move across, protected in transit by Android.

7. Encrypted backups and protected PDFs

You can export a backup file, or a copy of a document, protected with a password you choose. Backups are encrypted with a key derived from that password. We cannot recover the password: if you forget it, that backup cannot be opened. You are asked to type the password twice to avoid typing mistakes. A password-protected PDF is only as strong as its password and the program that opens it.

8. Sharing, printing and saving copies

When you share, print or save a copy, ScanPro hands the file to Android or to the app you choose, using a temporary permission for that one file. From then on, the privacy practices of that app or service apply. Temporary files made for sharing are cleared automatically once they are a day old.

9. Recycle bin

Deleted documents go to a Recycle bin on your device so you can restore them. They are removed for good after the retention period set in Settings (7 days unless you change it to 14 or 30), or when you empty the bin or delete an item permanently. Copies you exported, shared or saved elsewhere are not affected.

10. Notifications and background work

Saving a large scan or import can continue in the background; Android shows a notification while it does, and a notification when a scan could not be saved or a Recycle bin item is about to expire. You can turn the Recycle bin reminder off in ScanPro's Settings, and all notifications off in Android settings.

11. Messages you send us

"Send feedback" and "Report an issue" open your email app with a message already filled in. An issue report includes your app version, phone model, Android version, screen and memory details, recent app events, and the last crash if there was one. The app takes folder paths, links to other apps' files and exported file names out of it first, and says what it contains. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app, and you can read and edit it before then. We use what you send only to answer you and to fix the problem.

12. What ScanPro does not collect

ScanPro does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, call history, text messages or access to all files. It has no account system, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. The diagnostics in section 4 come from Google's libraries, not from ScanPro.

13. Security

ScanPro keeps your data in Android's private app storage, shares files only through temporary permissions, offers an optional biometric lock, and encrypts backups with a password. No software is perfectly secure. Keep Android up to date and protect your phone with a strong screen lock.
